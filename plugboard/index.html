Why Plugboard

Free, all of it

No account, no trial that runs out, no Pro tier holding back features. MIT licensed.

Small and fast

A small app that opens in a moment and stays quick, without eating your disk or memory.

Nothing phones home

No analytics, no crash reporter. It talks to your databases, your SSH servers and the update check — that’s it.

Two locks on production

Read-only is enforced by the app and by the database session itself, so a write that slips past one still meets the other.

Hard to break production by accident

Plugboard assumes the database you are connected to might matter.

  1. Read-only connections, on by default for anything tagged Production — refused in the app and in the database session itself.

  2. Writes to Production ask first and show exactly what will run.

  3. Grid edits are applied in one transaction: every change lands, or none does.

  4. A write is never re-run behind your back after a dropped connection.

  5. A changed SSH host key is refused until you have seen and trusted the new fingerprint.

Check what you download

Every release ships SHA-256 checksums and signatures, and the in-app updater refuses a build that fails either check. To check an installer yourself, put it next to SHA256SUMS.txt from the same release and run:

The builds aren’t signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so the first launch shows a warning. Here is what to click.

Older versions and their checksums are on GitHub.

macOS shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt --ignore-missing
Linux sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt --ignore-missing
Windows Get-FileHash .\installer.exe -Algorithm SHA256

What leaves your machine

The complete list. There is no analytics endpoint, so there is nothing to opt out of.

  • The databases you connect

    PostgreSQL, MySQL, MariaDB or a SQLite file — directly or through your SSH tunnel.

  • Your SSH servers

    A changed host key is refused until you have seen and trusted it.

  • GitHub, at launch

    To look for a new version. Install it yourself or let it update on its own.

Where your data lives

On your disk, in formats you can open without the app.

  • Passwords in the system keychain

    Keychain, Windows Credential Manager or Secret Service — never a file on disk.

  • Connections in a plain JSON file

    In your profile folder, where you can read it, back it up or delete it.

FAQ

Short answers to what people ask most.

Ask on GitHub
01 macOS says the app can’t be opened. What now?

The builds aren’t signed by Apple yet. Open it once, then click Open Anyway in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security. You only do this once.

02 Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”.

The builds aren’t signed by Microsoft yet. Click More info ▸ Run anyway. Updates the app installs itself don’t ask again.

03 What does Plugboard connect to?

Your databases, your SSH servers and, at launch, GitHub to look for updates — nothing else.
