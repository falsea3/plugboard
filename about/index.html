About us

Small tools, built in the open, in our spare time.

We build them for ourselves first.

Relay and Plugboard started as the tools we wanted on our own machines: fast, quiet, and ours. We use them in our own work every day, so every release has to get past us before it gets to you.

If something annoys us, it gets fixed. If a feature would need an account, a server or a tracking pixel, it doesn’t get built. We share the apps because it turns out other developers want the same thing — and that is the whole business plan, for now.

How we work

In our spare time

Relay and Plugboard aren’t a startup. We don’t make money from them, for now, and they stay free and MIT licensed.

In the open

The code, the issues and the release notes are public. Small fixes are welcome without asking first.
